About

I like going fast, on the track and in everything else.

Track taught me that progress comes from small things done every day. A tenth of a second can take months. I try to bring that same patience to school, where I've spent the last few years doing math and science research, and to music: violin in orchestra, guitar when I want to play for fun.

Lately I've gotten into marketing and building things on the web, because I want to start a business. I don't have the idea locked in yet, so for now I'm learning as much as I can and testing small ideas. This website is part of that, and I keep adding to it as I go.

Things I care about: showing up every day, asking why things work, and making real things instead of just planning them.
